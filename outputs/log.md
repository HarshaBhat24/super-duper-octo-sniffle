# Naming Convention

`outputs/YYYY-MM-DD_company_role.md`

Examples

- `outputs/2026-08-19_google_appsec.md`
- `outputs/2026-08-22_zepto_security_engineer.md`
- `outputs/2026-09-01_cloudflare_redteam.md`

Keep the role name lowercase with underscores. Keep the company name lowercase.

# Notes
